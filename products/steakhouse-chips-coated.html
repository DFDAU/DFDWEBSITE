Steakhouse Chips Coated

For those who crave a more substantial bite, our Steakhouse Chips Coated offer the perfect balance of thickness and crunch. Gluten-free and delicious, they stay crispy so you can enjoy every moment.

Cook Time: 3–4 Mins

Preparation Tips

  • Cook from frozen — do not defrost.
  • Deep fry. Preheat oil to 180°C.
  • Do not overcook.
  • Cook until a light golden colour or time specified.
  • Enjoy any time with your favourite condiments.

Store frozen. Do not refreeze once thawed. Shelf life is 24 months from the production date.
