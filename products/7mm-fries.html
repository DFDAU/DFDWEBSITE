Shoestring Thin Cut Frozen

7mm Fries

The shoestring chips are one of the most attractive French fries on the market. They are the longest chips, the fastest in cooking time than all the other regular fries! They can either be eaten by itself or as a side to a main. Perfect for restaurants and cafes.

Cook Time: 2–3 Mins

Preparation Tips

  • Cook from frozen — do not defrost.
  • Deep fry. Preheat oil to 180°C.
  • Do not overcook.
  • Cook until a light golden colour or time specified.
  • Enjoy any time with your favourite condiments.

Store frozen. Do not refreeze once thawed. Shelf life is 24 months from the production date.
