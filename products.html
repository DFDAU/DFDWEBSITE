How To Cook & Store

  • Cook from frozen — do not defrost.
  • Deep fry. Preheat oil to 180°C.
  • Do not overcook — cook until a light golden colour, or the time specified for that cut.
  • Store frozen. Do not refreeze once thawed. Shelf life is 24 months from the production date.
  • Enjoy any time with your favourite condiments.
