About Us

Built to keep your business supplied — reliably, every time.

We're a regional food distributor dedicated to helping restaurants, retailers, and institutions get the products they need, when they need them.

Our Story

A distribution partner focused on consistency

Direct Food Distribution was started with a simple goal: make it easier for foodservice businesses to get fresh, quality products without the guesswork. We work with growers, producers, and manufacturers to build a catalog that covers everything from fresh produce to pantry staples.

Today we support restaurants, grocers, caterers, schools, and other institutions across the region, with a focus on dependable delivery and responsive service — because a missed order affects your business, not just ours.
